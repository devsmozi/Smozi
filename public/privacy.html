Privacy Policy for SMOZI: Premium Block Puzzle

Effective Date: October 2026

At SMOZI, accessible from mobile application packages and web deployments, we prioritize the privacy of our players. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is gathered and how it is utilized.

1. Information Collection and Storage

SMOZI operates on an offline-first, client-side model. All gameplay progress, high scores, coin balances, level completions, and equipped customization themes are stored locally on your device via standard browser storage / local sandboxed storage.

  • We do not collect or transmit your personal identifiable information (PII).
  • We do not record location, contacts, phone state, or camera data.
  • All game saves are kept safely on your device.

2. Device Hardware Permissions

  • Vibration (Haptics): Used exclusively to deliver tactile physical feedback during piece placements and line combos.
  • Audio: Procedural Web Audio API is generated directly on your device. No microphone permission is requested or used.
  • Screen Wake Lock: Used to temporarily prevent the display from sleeping during an active puzzle round.

3. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

SMOZI does not target or knowingly collect any personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The game contains no chat rooms, social feeds, or personal data entry points.

4. Third-Party Analytics & Advertising

The game core does not embed third-party advertising trackers or invasive analytics SDKs. Any in-game currency (coins, gems) is earned exclusively through puzzle gameplay milestones, achievements, and daily rewards.

5. Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data. You may reset or clear all game data at any time via the in-game Settings menu or by clearing the app data in your device settings.
